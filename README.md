One image that sums up the world news of **Thursday, 19 November 2026**, made for the [make.fun daily news-image contest](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261119).

The poster leads with the day's biggest story — **GTA VI is out**, after 13 years of waiting, with a Metacritic 99, roughly 17 million copies and $1.65 billion already, 2.3 million watching on Twitch and leave requests up 32% worldwide — and rounds out the front page with five more of the day's stories:

1. **Summit in Shenzhen** — Trump, Xi and Putin hold the first-ever trilateral summit, Ukraine topping the agenda.
2. **World Court rules** — the ICJ decides on Myanmar's alleged genocide of the Rohingya (Gambia v. Myanmar).
3. **Tankers on fire** — three oil tankers hit mines and catch fire east of the Strait of Hormuz.
4. **Bangladesh burns** — Muhammad Yunus's ancestral home is torched as violence spreads to 28 districts.
5. **Climate talks** — COP31 ministers meet in Antalya, racing to unblock a final deal.

Plus: a Van Gogh sells for a record $190.4M, and Hakeem Jeffries is set to become the first Black US House Speaker.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` — the exact bytes the judge sees. The image is 1024×1024 PNG, about 153 KB, well inside the contest limits of 1024×1024 and 1 MiB.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
# writes out/entry.png
```
